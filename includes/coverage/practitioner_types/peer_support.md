# Peer Support

## Coverage Summary

Support grounded in lived experience, mutual understanding, encouragement, and human connection.

## Coverage Page Copy

Peer Support on SerenitySpaces creates room for people to be met by someone who understands aspects of their experience from the inside.

Peer support specialists may help clients feel less alone, process challenges conversationally, build coping routines, reflect on lived experience, and stay connected during difficult seasons. The value is not clinical authority, but the steady presence of someone who can say, “I understand this kind of terrain,” while still respecting boundaries and scope.

SerenitySpaces supports peer work through secure sessions, optional voice or video, encrypted notes, client history, inbox messaging, crisis resource signposting, goals, and action items. This makes it possible to provide warm human support while still maintaining structure and accountability.

Peer Support is not therapy and should not be presented as diagnosis or clinical treatment. When a client needs clinical care, crisis response, or specialized intervention, referral to an appropriate licensed or emergency resource is part of responsible support.
