# Grief Support

## Coverage Summary

Compassionate support for loss, mourning, remembrance, transition, and learning how to live with absence.

## Coverage Page Copy

Grief Support on SerenitySpaces gives clients a place to speak honestly about loss without being rushed toward closure.

Grief support practitioners may help clients process bereavement, life transitions, anticipatory grief, pet loss, estrangement, identity loss, or major changes that leave an emotional absence behind. The work often centers on witnessing, meaning-making, memory, ritual, and helping the client continue living without pretending the loss is small.

SerenitySpaces supports grief work through encrypted notes, session series, client reflections, message highlights, pinned memories, action items, and private between-session messaging. The platform can preserve the emotional thread of the work while keeping sensitive details protected.

Grief Support is not the same as clinical treatment for complicated grief, trauma, suicidality, or severe depression. Practitioners should refer clients to licensed or emergency support when the client’s needs exceed non-clinical grief care.
