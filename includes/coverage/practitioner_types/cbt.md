# Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

## Coverage Summary

Structured clinical work focused on thoughts, behaviours, patterns, skills, homework, and measurable change.

## Coverage Page Copy

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy on SerenitySpaces is a licensed clinical practice type for practitioners qualified to provide CBT within their professional scope.

CBT often focuses on the relationship between thoughts, emotions, behaviours, symptoms, and practical change. Practitioners may use structured homework, thought records, behavioural experiments, skills practice, goals, symptom tracking, and review of progress between sessions.

SerenitySpaces supports CBT through action items, goals and milestones, encrypted notes, intake forms, session recaps, client history, between-session messaging, and AI-assisted structured notes when enabled and consented to. The platform’s practical workflow tools are well suited to CBT’s emphasis on structure and accountability.

CBT should only be offered under this category by appropriately licensed or authorized professionals. Clients who require crisis intervention, emergency support, or specialist care should be referred according to clinical standards.
