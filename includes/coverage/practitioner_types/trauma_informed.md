# Trauma-Informed Support

## Coverage Summary

A cross-cutting support approach focused on safety, choice, pacing, consent, and avoiding re-traumatization.

## Coverage Page Copy

Trauma-Informed Support on SerenitySpaces is best understood as a practice approach that can sit alongside other roles.

A trauma-informed practitioner pays attention to safety, consent, pacing, emotional overwhelm, power dynamics, triggers, grounding, and the client’s right to choice. This does not automatically make the work clinical trauma therapy. It means the practitioner aims to avoid re-traumatization and to support the client with care, clarity, and respect.

SerenitySpaces supports trauma-informed work through text-only and voice-only options, consent-aware AI controls, encrypted notes, session pacing tools, crisis resources, private reflections, and the ability to keep sessions structured without forcing exposure or disclosure.

Trauma-Informed Support should not be confused with licensed trauma treatment unless the practitioner is properly qualified and licensed. Clients with active trauma symptoms, crisis risk, dissociation, abuse concerns, or clinical needs may require referral to licensed care or emergency services.
