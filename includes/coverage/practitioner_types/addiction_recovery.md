# Addiction Recovery

## Coverage Summary

Support for recovery, accountability, relapse reflection, sober routines, and rebuilding life around stability.

## Coverage Page Copy

Addiction Recovery support on SerenitySpaces gives clients a private, structured place to work on sobriety, relapse prevention, accountability, and life after addiction.

Recovery practitioners may help clients identify triggers, reflect on patterns, build routines, celebrate milestones, process relapse without shame, and maintain connection during vulnerable periods. This role can be especially important when clients need ongoing encouragement and practical support between formal treatment or community meetings.

SerenitySpaces supports recovery work through goals and milestones, action items, encrypted notes, crisis resources, between-session inbox messaging, session histories, and optional intake forms. These tools help practitioners track progress while keeping the client’s information private.

Addiction Recovery support is not a substitute for medical detox, licensed substance-use treatment, emergency care, or crisis intervention. Practitioners should refer clients to appropriate clinical or emergency resources when risk, withdrawal, self-harm, or medical need is present.
