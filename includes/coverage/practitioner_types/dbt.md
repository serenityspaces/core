# Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT)

## Coverage Summary

Clinical support for emotion regulation, distress tolerance, interpersonal effectiveness, mindfulness, and skills practice.

## Coverage Page Copy

Dialectical Behaviour Therapy on SerenitySpaces is a licensed clinical practice type for professionals qualified to provide DBT-informed or DBT-based care.

DBT commonly focuses on skills for emotion regulation, distress tolerance, mindfulness, interpersonal effectiveness, safety planning, and behaviour tracking. It often requires strong structure, between-session practice, diary-style monitoring, and careful attention to risk and crisis boundaries.

SerenitySpaces supports DBT through encrypted notes, action items, goals, session histories, client messaging, crisis resource signposting, session recaps, and AI-assisted documentation when enabled and consented to. These tools can support DBT-style accountability and skills reinforcement.

DBT should only be offered under this category by properly qualified clinicians. Because DBT may involve higher-acuity clients, practitioners must maintain appropriate risk protocols, referral pathways, emergency procedures, and jurisdiction-specific clinical obligations.
