# Spiritual Direction

## Coverage Summary

Companioning clients through spiritual discernment, contemplation, practice, meaning, and inner formation.

## Coverage Page Copy

Spiritual Direction on SerenitySpaces is for practitioners who accompany clients as they explore spiritual life, discernment, inner formation, contemplative practice, and relationship with the sacred.

A spiritual director may help a client notice patterns in prayer or meditation, reflect on vocation, process spiritual dryness, examine inner movements, discern next steps, or deepen a practice of attention and presence. This may be tradition-specific, interfaith, or broadly spiritual depending on the practitioner and client.

SerenitySpaces supports spiritual direction through quiet session spaces, voice or text participation, encrypted notes, session series, private reflections, and between-session messages for prayer, contemplation, or journaling prompts.

Spiritual Direction is not psychotherapy unless provided by a licensed clinician acting within scope. Practitioners should clearly state their training and tradition, avoid coercion, and refer clients when clinical, crisis, or medical needs are present.
