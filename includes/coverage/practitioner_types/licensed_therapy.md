# Licensed Therapy

## Coverage Summary

Licensed mental health care delivered by credentialed professionals within their legal and clinical scope.

## Coverage Page Copy

Licensed Therapy on SerenitySpaces is for credentialed mental health professionals providing care within their approved professional scope.

Licensed therapists may provide assessment, treatment planning, therapy sessions, clinical documentation, risk evaluation, and ongoing mental health support according to their licensure, jurisdiction, and professional standards. This category is intentionally gated to protect clients, practitioners, and platform operators.

SerenitySpaces supports licensed therapy through encrypted clinical notes, session histories, exports, intake forms, client portal access, AI-assisted note drafting when enabled and consented to, DPA controls, audit logging, session timeouts, and privacy-focused file handling.

Licensed Therapy requires proper credentials and operator review before clinical practice types are enabled. Practitioners remain responsible for their own legal, ethical, jurisdictional, professional, and documentation obligations.
